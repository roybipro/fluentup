FluentUp Account & Personal Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play User Data and Account Deletion requirements, you can delete your FluentUp account and all associated personal data using either of the two methods below:

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the FluentUp Android app.
  2. Navigate to the Profile tab at the bottom right.
  3. Scroll to Privacy, Terms & Account Deletion and tap Delete Account & Personal Data.
  4. Tap Confirm Delete. Your profile, lesson progress, quiz history, vocabulary cards, and practice sessions are deleted immediately.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained:
  • Deleted Permanently: User profile, email address, display name, lesson progress, quiz attempts, vocabulary notebook, speaking transcripts, grammar check history, and IELTS practice records.
  • Anonymized Compliance Audit Log: A non-reversible SHA-256 hash of the deletion request timestamp is kept for up to 90 days solely to demonstrate regulatory compliance.
