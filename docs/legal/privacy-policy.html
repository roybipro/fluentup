1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Data (Optional): Email address, display name, preferred explanation language (Bengali or English), self-selected or assessed English proficiency level, learning goals, and daily study target minutes.
  • Learning Activity & Progress: Lesson quiz scores, completed lessons, XP totals, study streaks, spaced-repetition vocabulary cards, grammar check inputs, and IELTS practice responses.
  • Voice & Microphone Data (User-Initiated Only): When you explicitly tap the Record button in the Speaking Lab, FluentUp requests android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO to record short voice clips locally on your device. FluentUp never records audio in the background.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information strictly to deliver personalized English lessons, schedule spaced-repetition vocabulary reviews, synchronize your progress across devices, and generate AI-assisted grammar, conversation, and unofficial IELTS practice feedback.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Processors

Provider Purpose Data Shared Security & Retention
Supabase (PostgreSQL & Auth) Authentication, session management, and cloud progress storage Email, hashed authentication credentials, profile, lesson progress, vocabulary Encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256); Row Level Security (RLS) enforced; deleted immediately upon account deletion.
Google Gemini API (via FluentUp FastAPI Server) Contextual AI conversation feedback, grammar explanations, and unofficial IELTS estimates Short text prompts/sentences submitted by the learner (without user email or account ID) Transmitted over HTTPS from our backend server; API keys never reside in the mobile app.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Local Guest Mode data remains on your Android device until you clear app storage or tap Delete Account & Personal Data in the Profile tab. Authenticated cloud data is retained while your account is active. When you request account deletion (either inside the mobile app at Profile → Delete Account & Personal Data or through our Public Account Deletion Portal), your profile and all associated database records are permanently deleted via cascading database constraints, and your active session tokens are immediately revoked.
